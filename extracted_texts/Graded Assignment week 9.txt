QUESTION 11
A manufacturer of electronics has a Cost of Production of ₹15,00,000. They have opening finished
goods worth ₹2,00,000 and closing finished goods worth ₹2,50,000. If the total sales revenue is
₹22,00,000, what is the Gross Profit?
Answer is Correct Score: 3 / 3
Options
FEEDBACK
Gross Profit is Sales minus COGS. COGS is calculated as Cost of Production + Opening Finished
Goods - Closing Finished Goods (15,00,000 + 2,00,000 - 2,50,000 = 14,50,000). Thus, Gross Profit
= 22,00,000 - 14,50,000 = 7,50,000.
A. 9,50,000
B. 6,50,000
C. 7,00,000
D. 7,50,000
Correct
