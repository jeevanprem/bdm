QUESTION 1
A company has a Debt-Equity ratio of 4:1. What does this primarily suggest to a potential lender?

Options
FEEDBACK

indicating a heavy reliance on external funds.

B. The company's assets are four times more valuable than its liabilities.
C. The company has significant unused borrowing capacity.
D. The company has a high financial risk due to heavy reliance on borrowed funds.

Options
FEEDBACK

available to pay interest.
