🎯 The 4-Step BDM Case Solver Framework

Follow this disciplined checklist when tackling any case scenario in tomorrow's exam:

STEP 1
Find the Scarce Constraint
Is it Budget Cap (₹120Cr / ₹50L), Time Bandwidth (6 months), or Physical Capacity?
STEP 2
Filter Irrelevant Costs
Ignore Sunk Costs (past non-refundable tickets/fees). Focus strictly on forward-looking trade-offs.
STEP 3
Apply the Formula
Net Margin = PAT/Rev; Quick Ratio = (CA - Inv)/CL; Opp Cost = Next Best Alternative value.
STEP 4
Check Intent & Traps
Is it Held for Sale (Inventory) or Held for Use (Fixed Asset)? Price cap below P* => Shortage.

Complete Mathematical Formula Cheat Sheet

Category Formula Interpretation & Benchmark
Opportunity Cost |ΔY / ΔX| (Slope of PPF) How many units of Good Y must be sacrificed to produce 1 unit of Good X.
Price Elasticity (Ep) (%ΔQd) / (%ΔP) = (ΔQ/Q) / (ΔP/P) |Ep| > 1: Elastic (luxuries); |Ep| < 1: Inelastic (necessities); |Ep| = 1: Unitary.
Cross-Price Elasticity (%ΔQx) / (%ΔPy) Exy > 0: Substitutes (Tea & Coffee); Exy < 0: Complements (Car & Petrol).
Gross Profit Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Profit earned directly from production before operating overheads.
Operating Profit (EBIT) Gross Profit - Operating Expenses Earnings before interest and tax; measures pure operational efficiency.
Net Profit Margin (Profit After Tax / Revenue) × 100 Percentage of each rupee of revenue that converts into bottom-line profit (XYZ Ltd = 8.44%).
Current Ratio Current Assets / Current Liabilities Short-term debt coverage. Ideal benchmark is approximately 2:1 (XYZ Ltd = 1.60).
Quick Ratio (Acid-Test) (Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid) / CL Instant liquidity excluding slow-moving inventory. Ideal benchmark is ~1:1 (XYZ Ltd = 1.37).
Debt-to-Equity Ratio Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity Financial leverage & solvency risk. Higher ratio (e.g. 4:1) implies high risk (XYZ Ltd = 0.20).
Return on Equity (ROE) (Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity) × 100 Net return generated on shareholders' invested capital (XYZ Ltd = 27.0%).
DuPont ROE Identity Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier Profitability × Efficiency × Leverage = 8.44% × 1.33 × 2.40 = 27.0%.
Earnings Per Share (EPS) Net Profit (PAT) / Number of Shares Earnings attributable to each individual common share (XYZ Ltd = ₹6.75).
P/E Ratio Market Price per Share / EPS How many times earnings investors are willing to pay (XYZ Ltd: 150 / 6.75 = 22.22).
Dividend Yield (Dividend per Share / Market Price) × 100 Cash dividend return on current market price (XYZ Ltd: 2 / 150 = 1.33%).
Market Capitalization Total Outstanding Shares × Market Price Total equity market valuation of the company (XYZ Ltd: 500 Cr × ₹150 = ₹75,000 Cr).
Inventory Turnover Cost of Materials (COGS) / Inventory How many times inventory is sold and replaced per year (XYZ Ltd: 28,000 / 3,500 = 8x).

1. Microeconomics & Scarcity (Weeks 1-4)

Needs vs Wants vs Desires Needs = Physical survival (food, shelter, water, clothing). Wants = Concrete desires to satisfy needs. Desires = Psychological wishes without ability to pay.
Opportunity Cost Value of the NEXT BEST alternative forgone. Sunk costs are past and non-recoverable — NEVER include sunk costs in decision making!
PPF Properties Concave (bowed-out) due to increasing opportunity costs. Inside = Inefficient/Underemployed. On curve = Productively efficient. Outside = Infeasible.
Cost Curves $TC = TFC + TVC$. $AFC$ drops continuously as $Q$ grows. $MC$ cuts both $ATC$ and $AVC$ at their exact lowest/minimum points!
Goods Classification (SEC) Search: Inspected before purchase (books, electronics). Experience: Evaluated after consumption (food, movies). Credence: Trust needed even after consumption (legal advice, medical surgery).
Elasticity & Revenue If Demand is Inelastic ($|Ep| < 1$): Price and Total Revenue move in the SAME direction ($P\uparrow \implies TR\uparrow$). If Elastic ($|Ep| > 1$): move in OPPOSITE directions ($P\downarrow \implies TR\uparrow$).
Cross & Income Elasticity $Exy > 0$: Substitutes. $Exy < 0$: Complements. $Ey > 0$: Normal goods ($Ey > 1$ Luxury, $0 < Ey \le 1$ Necessity). $Ey < 0$: Inferior goods.
Price Controls Price Ceiling (Cap) set below equilibrium price causes a persistent SHORTAGE and Deadweight Loss (as in R-Goura's student canteen case).

2. Financial Accounting & The Balance Sheet (Weeks 5-6)

Accounting Branches Financial: External, historical, GAAP/IFRS. Managerial: Internal, future-oriented (budgets/forecasts). Cost: Unit cost calculation.
Intent of Ownership Buildings built by a Real Estate developer for sale = INVENTORY (Current Asset)! Buildings used as factory by manufacturer = FIXED ASSET!
Accounting Equation $\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owner's Equity}$. Owner withdrawing cash for personal rent = Drawings (Equity $\downarrow$, Cash $\downarrow$).
Debit vs Credit DEAL (Debit increases: Drawings, Expenses, Assets). CLIP (Credit increases: Liabilities, Income, Capital/Equity).
Income Statement $\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{COGS}$. $\text{Operating Profit (EBIT)} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Operating Expenses}$. $\text{PAT} = \text{EBIT} - \text{Interest} - \text{Tax}$.

3. Financial Ratios & The XYZ Ltd Case (Weeks 7-8)

Liquidity Ratios $\text{Current Ratio} = CA / CL$ (Ideal ~2:1). $\text{Quick Ratio} = (CA - \text{Inventory} - \text{Prepaids}) / CL$ (Ideal ~1:1).
Solvency Ratios $\text{Debt-to-Equity} = \text{Total Debt} / \text{Equity}$. A high ratio like 4:1 signals high insolvency risk to lenders.
Cash Flow Statement Operating: Daily core business. Investing: Long-term PP&E purchases (buying machinery for cash = outflow). Financing: Shares, debt, dividends.
Profit vs Cash Flow A firm can report high net profits on accrual accounting and still go bankrupt if operating cash flows are negative!
XYZ Ltd Golden Numbers Net Margin = 8.44% | Operating Margin = 11.25% | Current Ratio = 1.60 | Quick Ratio = 1.37 | Debt/Equity = 0.20 | ROE = 27.0% | EPS = ₹6.75 | P/E = 22.22 | Div Yield = 1.33% | M-Cap = ₹75,000 Cr | OpEx = ₹34,500 Cr.
DuPont ROE Identity $\text{ROE} = \text{Net Margin} (8.44\%) \times \text{Asset Turnover} (1.33\text{x}) \times \text{Equity Multiplier} (2.40\text{x}) = \mathbf{27.0\%}$.

4. Management Accounting, Sales & Services (Weeks 9, 11, 12)

Responsibility Centers Cost: Costs only (IT, Maintenance). Revenue: Sales only. Profit: Revenue & Cost (Retail store). Investment: Revenue, Cost & Return on Capital (ROI/EVA).
7 Steps of Sales PPAPHCF: Prospecting (qualify via MAN: Money, Authority, Need) $\to$ Pre-approach $\to$ Approach (PSP technique) $\to$ Presentation (FAB) $\to$ Handling Objections $\to$ Closing $\to$ Follow-up.
4 Characteristics of Services IHIP: Intangibility (can't touch), Heterogeneity/Variability (quality differs), Inseparability (produced & consumed together), Perishability (empty seats can't be stored).
7Ps of Services Product, Price, Place, Promotion + People, Process, Physical Evidence.
SERVQUAL 5 Dimensions RATER: Reliability (promised service), Assurance (courtesy & trust), Tangibles (facilities/decor), Empathy (caring attention), Responsiveness (promptness).
Service Recovery Paradox Customers whose service failures are resolved exceptionally well often become MORE loyal than customers who never faced an issue. First step: Acknowledge & apologize sincerely.
